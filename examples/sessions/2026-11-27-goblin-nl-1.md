# Session: `goblin-nl-1` — Bram vs. the Goblin Ambush, in plain English (2026-11-27)

First playtest of the Stage 3/4 arena's interactive mode with natural-language input. Patrick played Bram
(`--as pc-bram`) and typed most orders in English. Claude translated each one into an engine intent, and the engine
resolved it. The AI played Talia and both goblins.

- **Trace:** [`2026-11-27-goblin-nl-1.trace.json`](2026-11-27-goblin-nl-1.trace.json). `bin/srd-arena replay` on it
  reports `deterministic_match: true`.
- **Seed:** `0`, chosen on purpose.
- **Command:**
  ```sh
  bin/srd-arena play goblin-ambush --as pc-bram --seed 0 \
    --nl-driver anthropic:claude-opus-5-5 --out-trace ~/arena-trace.json
  ```
- **Translator:** Claude Opus 5.5, signed in through the Anthropic CLI (`ant auth login`), no API key. This needs the
  Anthropic driver fix in [PR #23](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/pull/23). Before it, every
  `anthropic:` request was rejected because the driver sent `temperature`.
- **Seats:** Bram is the human. Talia, Goblin Scout A and Goblin Scout B are `heuristic-tactical`.

## Setup

| | HP | AC | Start | Initiative |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|
| **Bram Ironhand** (Fighter 1, human) | 12 | 19 | (0,0) | 19 |
| Talia Spark (Wizard 1) | 8 | 12 | (0,15) | 11 |
| Goblin Scout B (goblin warrior) | 10 | 15 | (25,15) | 7 |
| Goblin Scout A (goblin warrior) | 10 | 15 | (20,0) | 5 |

Turn order: Bram → Talia → Goblin Scout B → Goblin Scout A.

## Play

"NL" marks an order typed in English and translated by Claude. Every other order was a numbered menu choice or a
command.

### Round 1
- **Bram (NL).** He threw a javelin at "the one directly in front". Claude chose the correct target and weapon:
  `attack goblin-1 javelin`.
  - The attack was a natural 1: 1 + 5 = 6 vs AC 15, a **miss**.
  - Bram **rerolled with Heroic Inspiration** (`r`): 13 + 5 = 18, a **hit** for 4 piercing (1d6 + 3, the d6 rolled 1).
    Scout A went from 10 to 6. **Slow** took 10 ft off its speed.
- **Bram (NL), "Got him!"** It was meant as a remark, but Claude turned it into `move` to (15,0), 5 ft from Scout A.
  Its rationale said the line *"may be a celebration rather than a new action"*. See
  [#24](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/24).
- **Bram** ended his turn (menu).
- **Talia** cast **Mage Armor** on herself: AC 12 → 15.
- **Goblin Scout B** fired its shortbow at Talia: 2 + 4 = 6 vs AC 15, a **miss**.
- **Goblin Scout A** swung its scimitar at Bram: 7 + 4 = 11 vs AC 19, a **miss**.

### Round 2
- **Bram** attacked Scout A with his longsword (menu): 18 + 5 = 23 vs AC 15, a **hit** for 6 slashing (1d8 + 3).
  Scout A went from 6 to 0 and was **defeated**. **Sap** then triggered on the defeated goblin
  ([#27](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/27)).
- **Bram (NL).** He asked to block or interpose for Talia. Bram has no such action, so Claude substituted `move` to
  (25,10), next to Scout B, and ran it ([#24](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/24)).
- **Bram** ended his turn (menu).
- **Talia** cast **Magic Missile** at Scout B. Its three darts hit for 3, 5 and 3 force (each 1d4 + 1), taking Scout B
  from 10 to 7, 2 and 0. Scout B was **defeated**.

## Outcome

**Victory in 2 rounds.** Bram finished at 12/12 HP and Talia at 8/8. The goblins made 2 attacks and both missed.
Bram made 2 attacks plus one reroll, and both hits landed. Talia spent both of her level-1 slots. Bram's Heroic
Inspiration was spent.

| Composite | Outcome | Rules legality | Tactical | Coordination |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **96.35** | 99.0 | 100.0 | 94.0 | 88.0* |

\*Coordination counts party `table_talk`, and part of Bram's chat was written by the translator, not by Patrick
([#25](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/25)).

**Score note (2026-11-27, after [#30](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/pull/30)):** Re-scored with the new
contribution-based scorer, every sub-score is **unchanged** (composite 96.35, coordination 88.0): all 4 party turns
were productive, so contribution is 1.0. Removing Bram's 3 invented lines also leaves coordination at 88.0. Talia's
own callouts on 2 of the 4 productive turns already hit the communication cap, so the invented chat didn't move this
session's score. It could in sessions where the human seat carries most of the talk. The fix for that is tracked in #25
([PR #35](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/pull/35)). The trace's stored scorecard and digest were refreshed for the new scorer's added metrics
(`contribution`, `productive_talk_turns`, `effective_help_actions`). Game events are untouched, and
`bin/srd-arena replay` reports `deterministic_match: true`.

The run had 14 actions and **0 rules violations**. No state was edited by hand, unlike crypt-1, which needed four
manual interventions. Claude made 3 translation calls: 3,577 tokens in total, all accepted as legal on the first
try.

## Rules check

Every roll was checked against SRD 5.2:

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Bram's javelin and longsword +5 to hit (STR +3, proficiency +2); damage 1d6+3 and 1d8+3 | ✓ |
| Goblin warrior: AC 15, HP 10, shortbow and scimitar +4 | ✓ |
| Heroic Inspiration (2024): reroll right after the roll and keep the new result | ✓ (`reroll` intent; this was a hand edit in crypt-1, issue #7) |
| Mage Armor: 13 + DEX (+2) = 15 | ✓ |
| Magic Missile: 3 darts of 1d4 + 1 force, all auto-hit | ✓ (3, 5, 3) |
| Slow: −10 ft speed until the start of Bram's next turn; the effect expired at that point | ✓ |
| Sap on a target the hit dropped to 0 HP | ✗ minor ([#27](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/27)) |

## Natural-language translation

| Seq | Player typed | Claude's intent | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A javelin at the goblin "directly in front" | `attack goblin-1 javelin` | ✓ correct target and weapon |
| 3 | "Got him!" | `move` to (15,0) | ✗ not an order; should have done nothing ([#24](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/24)) |
| 12 | Block/interpose for Talia | `move` to (25,10) | ✗ impossible request; an action was substituted instead of explaining ([#24](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/24)) |

What the player typed is reconstructed from Claude's rationale. The trace doesn't store player input
([#26](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/26)).

The translator also wrote party chat in Bram's voice that Patrick never typed, for example *"Talia, stay behind me,
it'll have to get past me first!"* ([#25](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/25)).

**Why it happens:** `_build_nl_system_prompt()` requires exactly one legal intent, and its response format has a
required `table_talk` field but no way to say "no action" or ask a question.
