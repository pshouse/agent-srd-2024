# Session: `crypt-play-1`: Dorian in the Crypt of Ghouls (2026-11-27)

Second human playtest of the arena's interactive mode, and the first on a Tier 2 scenario. Patrick played Dorian
Dawnshield, the Life Cleric (`--as pc-dorian`), using menu commands only, with no natural-language translator. The
AI (`heuristic-tactical`) played Aldric, Lyra and all four undead.

- **Trace:** [`legacy-engine/2026-11-27-crypt-play-1.trace.json`](legacy-engine/2026-11-27-crypt-play-1.trace.json).
  It was recorded as `2026-27-crypt-play-1.trace.json`, with the month missing, and renamed afterwards. It's kept in
  `legacy-engine/` because it no longer replays on `main` (see [Replay](#replay)). That folder is outside the
  `examples/sessions/*.trace.json` glob that `TestCommittedTracesReplay` and the replay viewer index use.
- **Seed:** `768`, chosen at random.
- **Command:**
  ```sh
  bin/srd-arena play crypt-of-ghouls --as pc-dorian --seed 768 \
    --out-trace examples/sessions/2026-27-crypt-play-1.trace.json --save /tmp/crypt-play-1.save.json
  ```
- **Engine:** a local checkout from **before** #39 / PR #44 (monster traits) was pulled. It also predates the #51 fix
  (PR #68).

## Setup

| | HP | AC | Start | Initiative |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Crypt Archer (skeleton) | 13 | 14 | (35,15) | 17 |
| **Dorian Dawnshield** (Life Cleric 5, human) | 43 | 16 | (5,15) | 17 |
| Crypt Ghast | 36 | 13 | (30,5) | 15 |
| Crypt Ghoul A | 22 | 12 | (25,0) | 14 |
| Aldric Vance (Champion Fighter 5) | 44 | 19 | (0,5) | 14 |
| Lyra Starweave (Evoker Wizard 5) | 32 | 11 | (0,20) | 5 |
| Crypt Ghoul B | 22 | 12 | (25,10) | 3 |

## Play

### Round 1
- **Crypt Archer** fired its shortbow at Lyra: 19 vs AC 11, a **hit** for 5. Lyra went from 32 to 27.
- **Dorian** cast **Bless** (1st-level slot) on Lyra, Aldric and himself, and called *"Target the Ghouls!"*. He then
  cast **Healing Word** (1st-level slot, bonus action) on Lyra for 8, which the cap held to her 32 max HP.
  **Two slotted spells in one turn is illegal in 2024** ([#52](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/52)).
- **Dorian** tried `move 20 100` (85 ft against a 30-ft speed). The engine rejected it and logged a rules violation;
  it was most likely a typo. He then moved to (20,10) and ended his turn.
- **Crypt Ghast** moved to (25,10), **Ghoul B's square**, and clawed Dorian: 19 vs AC 16, a **hit** for 11. Dorian
  went from 43 to 32. He made his concentration save (23 vs DC 10) and his paralysis save (14 vs DC 10).
- **Crypt Ghoul A** moved to (20,5) and clawed Dorian: 8 vs AC 16, a **miss**.
- **Aldric** moved to (20,10), **Dorian's square** ([#53](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/53)).
  He made two greatsword attacks on the Ghast: 17 and 27, both **hits** for 8 and 13. The Ghast went from 36 to 15.
- **Lyra** cast **Fireball** (3rd level, DC 15) on the Archer, the Ghast and Ghoul B, using one shared damage roll of
  27. The Archer failed its save and was **defeated**. The Ghast failed its save and was **defeated**. Ghoul B made
  its save and took 13, going from 22 to 9.
- **Crypt Ghoul B** clawed Dorian: 22, a **hit** for 3. Dorian went from 32 to 29. **He failed his concentration
  save (7 vs DC 10), so Bless ended.** He made his paralysis save (19).

### Round 2
- **Dorian** cast **Spirit Guardians** (3rd-level slot) with both ghouls inside it. Both made their DC 15 Wisdom
  saves (20 and 17), yet each took **18** (9 radiant + 9 necrotic). Ghoul A went from 22 to 4. Ghoul B went from 9 to
  0 and was **defeated**. The spell's prose gives a choice of radiant *or* necrotic, but the engine rolled both, so
  the damage was doubled ([#51](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/51)).
- **Crypt Ghoul A** clawed Dorian: 19, a **hit** for 4. Dorian went from 29 to 25. **He failed his concentration
  save (6), so Spirit Guardians ended.** He made his paralysis save (13).
- **Aldric** hit Ghoul A with his greatsword: 23 for 6, taking it from 4 to 0. Ghoul A was **defeated**.

## Outcome

**Victory in 2 rounds.** Aldric finished at 44/44 HP, Lyra at 32/32 and Dorian at 25/43. Dorian spent two of his
four 1st-level slots and one of his two 3rd-level slots. Lyra spent one of her two 3rd-level slots, on Fireball.

| Composite | Outcome | Rules legality | Tactical | Coordination |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **94.34** | 96.9 | 92.31 | 93.0 | 94.0 |

The legality score of 92.31 (12 of 13 decisions legal on the first try) comes entirely from the `move 20 100`
typo. The two real rules breaks, the second slotted spell and the shared squares, scored as legal because the
engine allowed them.

**The result overstates the party.** Under correct rules:
- Dorian has to choose between Bless and Healing Word in round 1.
- Spirit Guardians deals 9, not 18, on a successful save, which leaves Ghoul A at 13 instead of 4. Aldric's 6-damage
  hit no longer finishes it, so the fight runs to at least round 3.
- Nobody can end a move in an occupied square, so the Ghast and Aldric both stop somewhere else.

## Rules check

`python3 scripts/audit_arena_trace.py` ran 164 checks across 27 rules and found **0 failures**. It covers attack math,
saves, Fireball, healing, concentration DCs, ghoul paralysis and resistances. It does not cover slots per turn,
square occupancy or spell damage types, which is why it missed all three bugs below. Each of these was checked by
hand:

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Ghoul and Ghast claws: paralysis CON save DC 10 on a hit; the creature is paralyzed only if the save fails | ✓ |
| Concentration DC = max(10, half damage); Bless ended on a failed save (7), and Spirit Guardians ended on 6 | ✓ |
| Fireball: 8d6 at 3rd level, DC 15, one damage roll for all targets, half on a success (27 → 13) | ✓ |
| Healing Word capped at max HP | ✓ |
| One spell with a spell slot per turn (Bless + Healing Word) | ✗ [#52](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/52) |
| Spirit Guardians: 3d8 radiant *or* necrotic, half on a success | ✗ deals both types [#51](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/51) |
| Can't end a move in an occupied square (Aldric on Dorian; Ghast on Ghoul B) | ✗ [#53](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/53) |
| Spirit Guardians damages creatures already inside the Emanation when it's cast | Adjudication: the engine treats "the Emanation enters a creature's space" as true on cast. That's a defensible reading, so no issue was filed. |

## Replay

On the engine at `cd27177` (just after #39), `bin/srd-arena replay` reported **`deterministic_match: false`**, but
the game itself replayed identically. Every action, roll, event, HP value and position matched. The only difference
was a `traits_ir` field on the Ghast, which the newer engine adds (#39 / PR #44). The trace was recorded on an older
checkout, and `schema_version` didn't change, so replay can't tell version skew from real nondeterminism
([#66](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/66)).

From PR #68 (the #51 fix) onward, replay also differs at action 20. That's expected: Spirit Guardians now deals one
damage type, so the recorded 18-damage events are no longer what the engine produces. The trace is kept as a record
of the bugs it found, not as a replay fixture.

## Issues

- [#51](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/51) Spells with either/or damage are compiled as additive
  damage (Spirit Guardians deals 6d8). **Fixed** in [PR #68](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/pull/68).
- [#52](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/52) Enforce the one-spell-slot-per-turn rule
- [#53](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/53) Block ending a move in an occupied square. A comment
  adds the enemy-side case: the Ghast on Ghoul B.
- [#66](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/66) Replay reports engine/state-shape version skew as
  nondeterminism (new from this session)
