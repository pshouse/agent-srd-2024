# Session: `wyrm-web-1`: Aldric in the Wyrmling Lair, in the browser (2026-11-27)

First playtest of the local browser tabletop, `bin/srd-web` from [PR #82](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/pull/82),
and the first human game of `wyrmling-lair`. Patrick played Aldric Vance, the Champion Fighter (`--as pc-aldric`),
by clicking squares, combatants and menu items. The AI (`heuristic-tactical`) played Lyra, Dorian, Kaelen and all
four enemies.

- **Trace:** [`legacy-engine/2026-11-27-wyrm-web-1.trace.json`](legacy-engine/2026-11-27-wyrm-web-1.trace.json).
  It was recorded **before** #52 (one spell slot per turn, PR #87) was enforced, and it's kept in `legacy-engine/`
  because it no longer replays on `main` (see [Replay](#replay)). That folder is outside the
  `examples/sessions/*.trace.json` glob that `TestCommittedTracesReplay` and the replay viewer index use, so the
  game isn't listed in the `#/replay` viewer.
- **Seed:** `598`, chosen at random.
- **Command:**
  ```sh
  bin/srd-web wyrmling-lair --as pc-aldric --seed 598 \
    --save /tmp/wyrm-web-1.save.json --out-trace /tmp/wyrm-web-1.trace.json
  ```

## Setup

| | HP | AC | Start | Initiative |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Kaelen Whisper (Halfling Thief 5) | 38 | 16 | (10,20) | 22 |
| Ember Magmin | 13 | 14 | (25,20) | 22 |
| Red Dragon Wyrmling | 75 | 17 | (30,10) | 20 |
| Cinder Hound B | 58 | 15 | (50,30) | 10 |
| Dorian Dawnshield (Life Cleric 5) | 43 | 16 | (5,15) | 8 |
| Lyra Starweave (Evoker Wizard 5) | 32 | 11 | (0,25) | 7 |
| **Aldric Vance** (Champion Fighter 5, human) | 44 | 19 | (0,0) | 6 |
| Cinder Hound | 58 | 15 | (25,0) | 2 |

## Play

### Round 1
- **Kaelen** hit the Wyrmling with his shortsword (Vex) and then his dagger (Nick, with Advantage from Vex): 75 → 65.
- **Ember Magmin** missed Kaelen.
- **Wyrmling** moved to (10,15). Kaelen's opportunity attack missed. Its **Fire Breath** hit Dorian (43 → 23) and
  Lyra (32 → 12); both failed their saves.
- **Cinder Hound B** moved to **(10,15), the Wyrmling's square**. Its Fire Breath hit Dorian (→ 17) and Lyra
  (→ 6); both made their saves.
- **Dorian** cast **Healing Word** on Lyra (→ 14) and **Bless** on Aldric, Kaelen and Lyra. Two slotted spells in
  one turn is illegal ([#52](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/52)). He then fell back to **(-25,-15)**,
  off any sensible map ([#84](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/84)). Two opportunity attacks
  followed; one hit him for 16 (→ 1 HP), but he kept concentration on Bless (20 vs DC 10).
- **Lyra** cast **Lightning Bolt** on the Wyrmling (→ 34, failed save) and both Hounds (B → 43 on a save; A → 27 on
  a failed save).
- **Aldric** moved to (5,15) and made two longsword attacks on the Wyrmling, both **hits** with Bless (18 and 28 vs
  AC 17), dealing 6 + 6 (→ 22). **Sap** triggered on both.
- **Cinder Hound** moved to **(10,15) as well**, making **three creatures on one square**
  ([#53](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/53)). Its Fire Breath dropped Lyra to 0 HP (unconscious,
  prone); Aldric made his save and took 7 (→ 37).

### Round 2
- **Kaelen** took the Magmin down to 1.
- **Magmin** hit Kaelen (→ 31).
- Fire Breath from the Wyrmling and Cinder Hound B, both aimed at Aldric. He made both saves and dropped to 27, then 21.
- **Dorian** used Healing Word to bring Lyra back up (→ 6) and cast Guiding Bolt, which missed. That's two slots in
  one turn again (#52).
- **Lyra** stood up and cast **Shatter**. The Magmin was **defeated**, and its Death Burst hit Kaelen (→ 27). The
  Wyrmling failed its save and dropped to 7; both Hounds made their saves.
- **Aldric** attacked the Wyrmling: 5 + Bless 1 = 13, a **miss**. He **rerolled with Heroic Inspiration** and got
  another 5, with Bless now 2, for 14, still a miss. The Bless die shouldn't have been rerolled
  ([#85](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/85)). His second attack, 17 + Bless 3 = 27, was a
  **hit** for 11, and the **Wyrmling was defeated**.
- **Cinder Hound** used Multiattack on Aldric, one hit for 16 (→ 5).

### Round 3
- **Kaelen** hit the Cinder Hound twice (→ 12).
- **Cinder Hound B** dropped Aldric to **0 HP** (unconscious, prone).
- **Dorian** used Healing Word to bring Aldric back up (→ 12) and cast Guiding Bolt, which missed (#52 again).
- **Lyra** cast Mage Armor. That was a wasted action mid-fight, an AI tactics problem rather than a rules bug.
- **Aldric**, still prone, crawled toward (0,10). Leaving Cinder Hound B's reach drew an opportunity attack with
  Advantage (prone, Pack Tactics). It hit for 12, putting him at **0 HP** again. The engine correctly stopped the
  move at (5,15). The tabletop kept showing "Moving to (0, 10)…" into the next round
  ([#83](https://github.com/pshouse/agent-srd-2024/issues/83)).
- **Cinder Hound** used Multiattack on Kaelen (→ 8).

### Round 4
- **Kaelen** hit the Cinder Hound twice (→ 3).
- **Cinder Hound B**'s Fire Breath dropped Kaelen to **0 HP**, even though he made his save.
- **Dorian** cast **Mass Healing Word** on Aldric (→ 12), Kaelen (→ 12) and Lyra (→ 18). His Guiding Bolt hit for
  15 and **defeated the Cinder Hound** (#52 again).
- **Lyra** hit Cinder Hound B with Magic Missile for 2 + 4 + 4 (→ 26).
- **Aldric** attacked from prone, with **Disadvantage** on both rolls: [9, 5] = 14 missed, and [20, 17] = 27 hit
  for 11 (→ 15, Sap).

### Round 5
- **Kaelen** stood up and hit Cinder Hound B twice (→ 5).
- **Cinder Hound B** used Multiattack on Aldric. Its first bite rolled **normally**, because Aldric being prone gave
  Advantage and Sap gave Disadvantage, so they cancelled. It hit for 13 (→ 0). The second bite had Advantage against
  an unconscious target and rolled a natural 20 for a critical hit of 24, giving Aldric **2 failed death saves**.
- **Dorian** used Healing Word to bring Aldric back up (→ 18), then cast **Sacred Flame**, a cantrip, so this turn was
  legal. Cinder Hound B failed its save and was **defeated**.

## Outcome

**Victory in 5 rounds**, after 5 revivals.

were made by hand:

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Prone attacker: Disadvantage on Aldric's attacks (R4) | ✓ |
| Prone target within 5 ft: Advantage on attacks against it (R3 opportunity attack, R5) | ✓ |
| Advantage + Disadvantage cancel to a normal roll (prone + Sap, R5 bite 1) | ✓ |
| Attack on an unconscious target within 5 ft: Advantage and an automatic crit; a crit at 0 HP = 2 failed death saves | ✓ |
| An opportunity attack that drops you to 0 stops the move where it triggered | ✓ |
| Standing from prone costs half Speed (menu: 15 ft) | ✓ |
| Healing at 0 HP revives (5 revivals) | ✓ |

## Replay

On `main` at `103cb62` (the engine this game was played on), `bin/srd-arena replay` reports
**`deterministic_match: true`**.

From PR #87 (#52, SRD 5.2 *one spell slot per turn*) onward, it no longer replays. In this game the AI's
Dorian spent two slots on each of his first four turns:

| Round | Dorian's two slotted spells |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Healing Word + Bless |
| 2 | Healing Word + Guiding Bolt |
| 3 | Healing Word + Guiding Bolt |
| 4 | Mass Healing Word + Guiding Bolt |

Under #87 the second cast of each turn is refused. The game diverges from round 1, and replay ends in a **draw**
instead of the recorded **party win**. The trace is kept as a record of this playtest and its findings, not as a
replay fixture.
