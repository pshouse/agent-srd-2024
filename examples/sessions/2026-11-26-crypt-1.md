# Session: `crypt-1` — Aldric vs. Cultist & Skeleton (2026-11-26)

First live playtest of the Stage 2 engine: one Level 1 PC through `bin/srd character` + `bin/srd encounter`,
driven by Claude Code with Patrick choosing Aldric's actions. Monster turns were played by the agent.

- **Build spec:** [`examples/characters/aldric-paladin-1.json`](../characters/aldric-paladin-1.json)
- **Final engine state:** [`2026-11-26-crypt-1.final-state.json`](2026-11-26-crypt-1.final-state.json)
  (contains two manual HP patches; see *Engine deviations* below)
- **Encounter seed:** `418711274` (drawn with `secrets.randbelow`)

## Encounter

Cultist (CR 1/8, 25 XP) + Skeleton (CR 1/4, 50 XP) = **75 XP, Moderate** for one Level 1 PC.

Starting positions: Aldric (0,0), Cultist (25,0), Skeleton (40,10).
Initiative: **Cultist 21, Skeleton 10, Aldric 7**.

### Round 1
- **Cultist** moves 20 ft to (5,0) and attacks with its Ritual Sickle: 15 vs AC 18, **miss**.
- **Skeleton** fires its Shortbow from 40 ft: 15 vs AC 18, **miss**.
- **Aldric** attacks the Cultist with his Longsword: nat 2, 6 vs AC 12, **miss**.
  - Aldric uses **Heroic Inspiration** to reroll. *(Done outside the engine: action restored by hand, new d20 = 7 passed
    via `--roll-overrides`. See #7.)* 11 vs AC 12, **miss**.
  - Aldric ends his turn and holds his position.

### Round 2
- **Cultist** attacks with its Sickle: 10 vs AC 18, **miss**.
- **Skeleton** fires its Shortbow: 14 vs AC 18, **miss**.
- **Aldric** attacks the Cultist with his Longsword: 8 + 4 = 12 vs AC 12, **hit** for 5 slashing (Cultist 9 → 4). Sap triggers.
  - **Divine Smite** (L1 slot): the engine spent the slot and dealt **no damage**. *(Rolled by hand, 2d8 = 3 + 7 =
    10 radiant, and applied to state. See #6.)* Cultist 4 → 0, **dead**.
  - Aldric moves 30 ft to (30,10), 10 ft from the Skeleton.

### Round 3
- **Skeleton** fires its Shortbow: 19 vs AC 18, **hit** for 6 piercing. Aldric 11 → **5**.
- **Aldric** moves 5 ft to (35,10) and attacks the Skeleton with his Longsword: 19 + 4 = 23 vs AC 14, **hit** for 9 slashing
  (Skeleton 13 → 4). Sap triggers.
  - **Divine Smite** (last L1 slot): the engine again dealt no damage. *(Rolled by hand, 3d8 = 1 + 4 + 4 = 9 radiant,
    including the +1d8 against undead, and applied to state.)* Skeleton 4 → 0, **destroyed**.
  - The encounter status becomes `ended`. The final `end_turn` still printed "Round 4: Aldric's turn begins." (#9)

## Outcome

**Victory.** Aldric ends at 5/11 HP, with 0/2 L1 slots, Lay on Hands 5/5, and Heroic Inspiration spent. He earns **75 XP**.
The enemies made 6 attacks and hit once. Aldric made 4 attacks (plus 1 reroll) and hit twice; both hits were smite kills.

## Engine deviations (manual interventions)

| # | Step | What the agent did by hand | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Round 1, Heroic Inspiration | Set `turn_resources.action = true`, re-ran the attack with `--roll-overrides [7]` | #7 |
| 2 | Round 2, Divine Smite | Rolled 2d8 radiant (`secrets`), set Cultist `hp=0, alive=false` | #6 |
| 3 | Round 3, Divine Smite | Rolled 3d8 radiant (undead), set Skeleton `hp=0, alive=false` | #6 |
| 4 | Character | Magic Initiate spells tracked on paper only | #8 |
